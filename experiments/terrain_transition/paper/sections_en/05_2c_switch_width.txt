In the switch-width (w) sweep (0.05-2.0 m; Figure 8), the switch's internal continuity improved steadily from 7.03e-4 m at w = 0.05 m to 1.74e-7 m at w = 1.0 m, but worsened slightly at w = 2.0 m (1.83e-5 m); this suggests that once the switch width approaches magnitudes near the contact radius, the switch begins to distort its own local curvature, and it shows that w roughly in the 0.3-1.0 m range is a suitable default for internal continuity near machine precision; the w = 0.3 m used in the production configuration provides a deliberate safety margin within this range.
