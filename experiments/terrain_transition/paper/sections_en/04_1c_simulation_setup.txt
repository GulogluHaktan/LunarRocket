This subsection collects in one place the simulation configuration shared by both the analytic (CPU) protocol and the real Isaac Lab/Isaac Sim training runs (Table 1). Physics is solved GPU-parallel on NVIDIA PhysX with a fixed step of 1/120 s (120 Hz); the policy produces actions at 60 Hz with decimation = 2, and each episode lasts 20 seconds, i.e. 1200 control steps. Lunar gravity (1.62 m/s^2), a low-restitution regolith-like contact material, and 512 concurrent environment instances were held fixed across all runs.

The vehicle and actuation parameters (mass, thrust envelope, physical and policy gimbal limits, TVC actuator gains) and the SAC hyperparameters were not changed among the three blending methods. The only quantity that varies from method to method is the fine-detail fade function and its associated parameters defined in Section 3.3 (the last group of Table 1; selected via the ISAACLAB_TERRAIN_BLEND_METHOD environment variable). This fixing is necessary so that the differences observed in Section 4 can be attributed solely to the blending method.

Because of a compute-budget constraint, the real training comparison was run with a shortened version of the full-scale (1200-iteration) configuration -- three independent seeds per method, roughly 5.73 million environment steps per run; this scale limitation is discussed separately in Section 3.6 and Section 4.3.
