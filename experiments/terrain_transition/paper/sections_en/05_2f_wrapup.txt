The common conclusion of the four analyses is that the continuity advantage of the proposed hybrid method is robust over a reasonable parameter range (rc = 1.0-4.0 m, w = 0.3-1.0 m), i.e. it is not overly sensitive to a particular fine-tuning. The only exception is that at very narrow switch widths (w <= 0.1 m) the internal continuity degrades noticeably; this once again confirms that the default used in the production configuration (w = 0.3 m) contains a deliberate safety margin.
