Table 3 shows the computational cost, in microseconds, of the fade formula itself (isolated from terrain sampling). Smoothstep has the lowest cost (93.1 microseconds); the Gaussian variants (as-shipped and calibrated) have a similar, mid-level cost (roughly 149-150 microseconds); the hybrid method has the highest cost (407.8 microseconds, roughly 4.3 times that of smoothstep), because it computes both the Gaussian and the smoothstep branches as well as the switch weight on every query.
