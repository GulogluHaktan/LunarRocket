In the contact-radius (rc) sweep of the hybrid method (1.0-4.0 m; Figure 7), the C0 discontinuity at the switch's own internal transition boundary (r = rc) stayed below 3e-4 m at all rc values, and the fade cost stayed constant regardless of rc (roughly 755-793 microseconds); this confirms that the cost stems not from where the switch is located but from both branches being computed in every case.
