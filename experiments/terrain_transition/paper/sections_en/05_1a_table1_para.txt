Table 2 summarizes, as a ratio relative to smoothstep, the headline continuity measurements aggregated over thirty seeds and isolating only the faded fine-detail component. The as-shipped Gaussian (gaussian_shipped, k = 1.0) produces on average 3192 times worse C0 (height) continuity and 535 times worse C1 (slope) continuity than smoothstep; both differences are extremely significant by the Wilcoxon signed-rank test (p = 1.86e-09, n = 30). The calibrated Gaussian (k = ln(1000)) largely removes this defect but still leaves measurably higher residual error than smoothstep (roughly 9.3x on C0, roughly 1.6x on C1). The hybrid method, being analytically identical to smoothstep at the r = R boundary (switch(R) = 0), showed zero difference on all thirty seeds; this is not a measurement result but an expected consequence of the method's definition.
