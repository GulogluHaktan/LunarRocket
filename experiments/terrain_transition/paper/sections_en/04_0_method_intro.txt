This section consists of six subsections: the experimental environment and task definition, the simulation setup shared by all runs, the terrain representation and the mathematical formulation of the blending problem, the analytic (CPU) evaluation protocol, the reinforcement learning method used (the task's formulation as a Markov decision process and the Soft Actor-Critic algorithm), and the real Isaac Lab/Isaac Sim training protocol. All three blending methods were evaluated first at the analytic/CPU level and then inside the real GPU-parallel physics simulation, under the same fixed transition radius and the same hard-truncation rule; this consistency makes the findings comparable across the two evaluation layers.
