Figure 4 shows the combined (macro + fine detail) height cross-section of the four method variants. At this scale the four curves almost exactly overlap, because the fine-detail amplitude (on the order of centimetres) is very small compared with the macro terrain scale (on the order of metres); the order-of-magnitude differences reported in Table 2 cannot be discerned by eye at this visual scale. This directly demonstrates why the "detail-only" isolation step defined in Section 3.4 is necessary: the seam defect emerges only when the fine-detail component is separated out.
