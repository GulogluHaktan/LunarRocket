The final success rate measured in the final ten-percent window was, as a method average, 27.8% +- 5.5% for smoothstep, 32.5% +- 5.1% for the calibrated Gaussian, and 26.8% +- 2.5% for the hybrid (Table 5). The Gaussian's mean is still the highest, but the standard deviations of the three methods overlap with each other. Table 6 summarizes the pairwise statistical tests of these differences: no pairwise comparison is statistically significant; on success rate, smoothstep-Gaussian p=0.20, smoothstep-hybrid p=1.00, Gaussian-hybrid p=0.40; on timeout rate, p=0.70, p=1.00, p=0.40 respectively; on harsh-landing rate, all three p>=0.70. All of the bootstrap 95% confidence intervals include zero (e.g. [-0.000, 0.104] for the Gaussian-hybrid success-rate difference -- borderline but containing zero).
