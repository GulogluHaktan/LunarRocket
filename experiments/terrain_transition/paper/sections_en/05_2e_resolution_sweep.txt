In the grid-resolution sweep (grid spacing roughly 0.31-1.27 m; Figure 10), the empirical convergence order (slope of the log-log linear regression) of the smoothstep and hybrid methods was measured at roughly 0.90, i.e. the error decreases as expected as resolution increases. For the calibrated Gaussian, a negative slope (roughly -0.36) was observed; however, this is not a physical "worsens as resolution increases" finding -- the calibrated Gaussian's error is already on the order of 1e-6 to 1e-7 m, close to a fixed truncation-residual noise floor set by the calibration constant, and this floor constitutes a measurement limit independent of grid resolution; this point is stated explicitly here to avoid a misleading physical interpretation.
