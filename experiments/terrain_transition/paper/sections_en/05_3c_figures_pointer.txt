Figure 11 shows all nine runs of the three methods on the same plot for the three main outcome metrics (success, timeout, harsh-landing rate); the nine curves being largely intertwined throughout training confirms that the difference among methods cannot be distinguished visually from the variance across seeds. Figure 12 shows, for the same nine runs, complementary training diagnostics such as curriculum difficulty, entropy coefficient, and xy-progress reward; the entropy coefficient following an almost identical convergence curve for all three methods indicates that SAC's exploration-exploitation balance operates independently of the choice of blending method. Figure 13 presents the final ten-percent window values as per-method scatter points and a mean +/- standard deviation bar; the wide overlap of the three methods' error bars is also clearly visible here.
